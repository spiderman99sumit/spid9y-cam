## OBS/creator community draft

I made Spid9y Cam after struggling to get my phone camera connected over USB. It's a free, open-source Android + Windows tool with a native hardware-H.264 capture path, a local browser control panel, direct OBS input, and optional OBS Virtual Camera output.

The native pipeline has been tested on a Redmi Note 9 Pro at 1920 × 1080 / about 30 fps. It is an early beta, not a universal-device promise. It needs USB debugging and the phone app kept visible; this release has no phone audio or OEM Night/HDR/AI processing. I'm looking for reproducible compatibility reports and setup feedback.

Source and downloads: https://github.com/spiderman99sumit/spid9y-cam

Post only where the community permits self-promotion. Start with technical feedback, not donation requests.
